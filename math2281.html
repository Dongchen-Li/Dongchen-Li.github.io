York University · SC/MATH 2281 · 3.00 credits

Models of Financial Economics

An introduction to the mathematical ideas behind financial contracts, their valuation, and risk management.

Course Overview

The course explores how no-arbitrage reasoning connects financial prices and hedging strategies. It introduces derivative contracts and models for valuing them, from discrete-time trees to the Black–Scholes framework.

Topics

  • Financial contracts: forwards, futures, and American and European options.
  • No-arbitrage and hedging: pricing relationships, put–call parity, and price bounds.
  • Valuation models: binomial and trinomial trees, risk-neutral valuation, and the Black–Scholes formula.
  • Interest rates: yield curves and interest-rate derivatives.
